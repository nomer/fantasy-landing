Fantasy Copilot · Effective August 30, 2026

Privacy Policy

Fantasy Copilot is an individual’s personal project. The owner is its only user.

Information collected

After the owner grants access through Yahoo OAuth, Fantasy Copilot receives OAuth tokens and data from Yahoo Fantasy leagues the owner belongs to. This data includes league settings, rosters, draft results, transactions, and player data.

How information is used

The information is used only to generate draft and roster recommendations for the owner. Fantasy Copilot does not sell or share it, and does not use advertising or analytics.

Storage and security

Fantasy data is stored on a private server. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest, remain server-side, and are never exposed to the browser.

Retention and revocation

Data is retained while the tool is in use. The owner can revoke access at any time from the Yahoo account’s connected-apps settings. After access is revoked, Fantasy Copilot can no longer read Yahoo data.

Fantasy data provided by Yahoo Fantasy.
