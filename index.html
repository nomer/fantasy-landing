Fantasy Copilot

in development Yahoo API access pending private · single user

A private assistant for my own Yahoo Fantasy Football leagues. It follows my draft, weighs the board against my personal rankings and strategy, and explains its suggestions — every actual pick and roster move happens in Yahoo, made by me.

Scope

  • Tracks my live Yahoo draft and suggests picks from my own rankings and draft strategy.
  • Estimates which players are likely to still be available at my next pick.
  • In season: waiver and free-agent analysis plus injury and depth-chart news for my roster.
  • Explains every recommendation; the final call is always mine, made in Yahoo.

Yahoo Fantasy integration

  • Connects through the official Yahoo Fantasy Sports API using OAuth, read-only.
  • Reads only leagues I’m a member of: league settings, rosters, draft results, transactions, and player data.
  • Never makes draft picks, roster moves, waiver claims, or trades on my behalf.
  • Uses data solely to generate recommendations for me. It is not shared, sold, or used for any other purpose.
  • Stores Yahoo tokens server-side, encrypted, and never exposes them to the browser.
